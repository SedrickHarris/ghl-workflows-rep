# 01 — Workflow Discovery

**Goal:** elicit enough from the client to design workflows that fit their actual business.

Capture answers in `clients/{{client}}/discovery.md`.

## Business context
1. What does the business do, in one sentence?
2. Who is the typical customer? (B2C / B2B, ticket size, sales cycle length)
3. What's the single most important outcome we should drive in the next 90 days?

## Current funnel
4. How do leads currently arrive? (paid, organic, referral, walk-in, calls)
5. What happens between "lead arrives" and "money changes hands"? Walk through it.
6. Where do leads most often drop off?
7. What's manual today that should not be?

## Tooling
8. Which HighLevel sub-account / location? (record the ID)
9. Which integrations are live? (calendars, payments, phone, email domain, ad platforms, CRMs)
10. Which phone numbers / sending domains are in use, and are they warmed?

## Compliance / policy
11. Any opt-in language already collected at lead capture?
12. Quiet hours / business hours for SMS?
13. Any regulated category? (healthcare, financial, legal — affects messaging)

## Constraints
14. Anything we **cannot** automate (must stay human)?
15. Any past automations that failed? Why?

## Output
Summarize at the end of `discovery.md`:
- Top 3 candidate workflows to build, ranked by impact × effort.
- Open questions to send back to the client.
